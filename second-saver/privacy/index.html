Second Saver Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Second Saver ("the app") is made by butterXfly. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Your financial records stay on your phone. There is no account, no login and no server of ours.
  • We never see, collect or sell your records.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads. The ads are non-personalised, which means they are not based on your interests.
  • Buying "Remove ads" turns the ads off.

Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter stays in the app's storage on your phone: income, spending, commitments, payments, wallets, categories, budgets, notes, settings, and any photos or places you attach. We have no copy of it and no way to read it.

If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it, unless you made a backup.

Backups and exports

You can make a backup file or export your transactions as a CSV file. These files are saved on your phone or wherever you choose to send them, for example a cloud drive, an email or a chat. Once a file leaves the app, the service you send it to handles it under its own privacy policy. A backup can be protected with a password that only you know. We can't recover it if you forget it.

Features that use your phone's permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can say no and still use the rest of the app.

  • Camera and photos: to attach photos, such as receipts, to your records. The photos are stored in the app on your phone.
  • Location: only when you tap "Use my current location" on a record. To turn the position into an address, the app uses your phone's built-in address lookup, which sends the position to Apple (on iPhone) or Google (on Android). The place is then saved with that record on your phone.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint: to unlock the app if you turn on the app lock. Your phone checks your face or fingerprint itself. The app only learns whether the check passed.
  • Notifications: for bill and backup reminders. These are scheduled on your phone. No reminder is sent from a server.

Exchange rates

If you use more than one currency, the app downloads current exchange rates from a free public service (open.er-api.com). The request contains only the currency code, such as "MYR". Like any internet request, it shows that service your phone's IP address. None of your records are sent.

Ads

The free version shows a banner from Google AdMob. We ask Google for non-personalised ads only, so the ads aren't chosen from your interests or browsing.

To show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad system may collect information about your device. This includes your IP address, device type and settings, your device's advertising ID (on Android), and how you interact with the ads. Google handles this under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your financial records are never shared with the ad system.

Europe, the UK and Switzerland: if you are in one of these places, the app asks for your consent before showing ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the app's settings.

On iPhone: the app does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Remove ads purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). They handle the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app receives only a confirmation that you bought it. After the purchase, the app no longer loads ads or the ad system.

Children

The app is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • You can view, change or delete any of your records in the app at any time.
  • You can turn off camera, photo, location and notification access in your phone's settings.
  • You can change your ad consent choice in the app (Europe, UK and Switzerland).
  • You can remove the ads by buying "Remove ads".
  • Deleting the app deletes everything stored in it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
